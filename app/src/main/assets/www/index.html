Scope & limitations

  • All 200 vehicles are local content shells sharing two procedural chassis: Derby and Sport.
  • Only Shatterline Coliseum and Skyline Airport are implemented. The other 53 world profiles are marked Coming Soon.
  • Customization is cosmetic: paint, accent, rim finish, underglow and decals. No body kits or new wheel geometry.
  • No nitro, online multiplayer, cloud saves, leaderboards, real-money store or premium currency.
  • Audio is synthesized in Web Audio; no sampled engine or music files are included.
